What We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email, purpose, and primary goal.

How We Use Data

  • To provide AI tutoring, coding, content, PDF, image, reel, and dashboard features.
  • To save previous work so you can continue later.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, debug failures, and improve product quality.
  • To process subscriptions, credits, invoices, refunds, and support requests.

AI Providers

Suhana AI may send your prompts and files to configured AI providers such as Gemini, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, and other generation providers. Do not submit secrets, passwords, private keys, or highly sensitive personal data.

Storage And Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as login sessions, CSRF protection, security headers, encrypted API-key storage when configured, and limited access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may request deletion of your account, saved work, uploaded files, or generated content by contacting support. Paid plan cancellation and refund rules are described in the Terms.
