Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes, absolutely. Cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard. No questions asked, no cancellation fees.

What payment methods do you accept?+

We accept all major cards, UPI, net banking, and wallets via Razorpay. All payments are fully secured and encrypted.

Do unused generations roll over?+

Monthly generations reset each billing cycle. Pro users get unlimited images so it rarely matters.

Is there a free trial for Pro?+

New users get a 7-day Pro trial free of charge. No credit card required to start your trial.

Can I use Suhana AI commercially?+

Yes. All content on Pro and Business plans is fully yours for commercial use including ads and client projects.

How does the API work?+

Business plan users get full REST API access with comprehensive docs to integrate our AI into your own apps.
